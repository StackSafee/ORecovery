Rules engine

The ranking policy ORecover applies when selecting a replacement patient. Rules are read from the hospital's approved scheduling protocol — never set by ORecover.

RUH-SURG-2024-03 · v3.2 · effective 2024-03-01

Short-notice replacement selection protocol

Owner: Surgical Services Committee · RUH Last reviewed: 2026-04-11 Signed off: Dr. L. Pelletier, VP Surgical Services
1
Clinical priority (CTAS-S)
Patients with higher clinical priority (lower number) are selected first. Priority 1 is immediate, Priority 4 is routine. ORecover will never advance a lower-priority patient over an available higher-priority patient.
priority ASC
2
Procedure and OR compatibility
The replacement patient's booked procedure must match the available OR time (duration, equipment, anesthesia profile). Any mismatch disqualifies the candidate automatically.
procedure.code === cancelled.procedure.code
3
Pre-operative readiness window
Pre-op assessment, consent, and medical clearance must be complete and within 30 days of the opening. Patients outside the window are not contacted; patients inside but older than 21 days are flagged for same-day re-check.
preOp.completedDaysAgo <= 30
4
Short-notice consent
Only patients who have explicitly opted in to the short-notice list are contacted. Opt-in is captured during pre-op and can be withdrawn at any time by the patient.
shortNotice.optedIn === true
5
Surgeon preference match (tie-breaker)
Within the same priority band, patients whose care plan names the cancelled surgeon are ranked above patients with "any surgeon" or a different preferred surgeon. This preserves continuity of care.
surgeonMatch DESC
6
Waiting time (tie-breaker)
Within the same priority and surgeon-match band, the patient with the longest waiting time is selected. This complies with the Saskatchewan Patient-First surgical access standards.
waitingDays DESC
✓
Human confirmation (non-negotiable)
ORecover never books a surgery. The scheduler must confirm the recommended patient before the OR slot is reassigned. Scheduler may override the recommendation with a documented reason, which is written to the audit trail.
manual_confirmation_required = true
